The Journey of Building Trust

The journey of Shree Ayyapan Blue Metals & Kamatchi Amman Hollow Blocks began in 1990 with a vision to provide reliable construction materials and build lasting relationships with customers.

Founded by Mr. Paramasivam K. and Mrs. Muniyammal P., our business was built on the values of hard work, honesty, dedication, and a commitment to quality. What started as a family business has grown into a trusted name in the construction materials industry, serving individuals, builders, contractors, and construction professionals across Theni and surrounding districts.

Over the years, we have remained committed to providing dependable materials that contribute to strong and lasting structures. Quality has been a fundamental part of our journey since 1990. We strive to support residential, commercial, and infrastructure construction projects by offering essential building materials with a focus on quality, reliability, and customer satisfaction.

Our Founders

Our founders, Mr. Paramasivam K. and Mrs. Muniyammal P., laid the foundation of this business with determination and a clear vision for the future.

Their dedication, perseverance, and commitment to customer satisfaction helped establish the values that continue to guide our business today. The principles they established remain at the heart of everything we do.

Preserving Legacy, Embracing Growth

Growing Through Generations

As the business continued to grow, the next generation stepped forward to carry on the founders' vision.

Today, Mr. Ramakrishnan P. and Mr. Selvapandi P., the sons of our founders, manage the business with the same dedication and commitment that shaped its beginnings. By combining the values of the previous generation with a forward-looking approach, they continue to strengthen the business and serve the changing needs of the construction industry.
